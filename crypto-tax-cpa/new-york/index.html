Crypto Tax Rules in New York

New York taxes crypto gains as capital gains, with top state rates reaching 10.9%. If you live in New York City, you may also be subject to local city income tax, pushing the combined top rate even higher. New York also requires crypto businesses operating in the state to hold a BitLicense, which carries specific tax and reporting implications for business owners.

Frequently Asked Questions: New York Crypto Taxes

Does New York City have a separate crypto tax?

Yes. If you live in New York City, you are subject to city income tax in addition to the state income tax. The combined top marginal rate can exceed 14% for high earners.

Is crypto mining taxable in New York?

Yes. Mining rewards are treated as ordinary income at the fair market value when received, subject to both NYS and potentially NYC income taxes.

What is the BitLicense and does it affect my taxes?

The BitLicense is a business license required for crypto businesses operating in New York. If you run a crypto business, compliance costs and specific reporting requirements may affect your tax situation.
