Crypto Tax Rules in Florida

Florida does not have a state income tax, meaning crypto investors here only face federal tax obligations. However, Florida does impose a corporate income tax on businesses, and there are specific state sales tax rules that can apply to NFT sales and certain crypto services. Florida's lack of state tax makes it a popular residency for active crypto traders.

Frequently Asked Questions: Florida Crypto Taxes

Does Florida tax crypto gains?

No. Florida has no state income tax, so crypto capital gains and ordinary income are not taxed at the state level. You only owe federal taxes.

Are NFTs subject to Florida sales tax?

Florida has specific sales tax rules that can apply to certain digital products and NFT sales. It's highly recommended to speak with a Florida CPA if you are actively trading or minting NFTs.

Does Florida tax crypto staking rewards?

No. Since Florida has no state income tax, staking rewards are not subject to state tax. They are, however, subject to federal income tax at the fair market value when received.
