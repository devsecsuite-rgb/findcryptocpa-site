Florida Crypto Tax Notes

Florida has no state income tax, making it one of the most tax-friendly states for crypto investors. Only federal capital gains tax applies.

Key rates and rules

  • No state income tax: Florida does not tax crypto gains at the state level
  • Federal capital gains: 0%, 15%, or 20% based on income bracket
  • Crypto-friendly regulation: Florida has passed favorable blockchain legislation

Enforcement notes

  • Form 1099-DA: All US exchanges report activity starting 2026
  • Residency matters: If you moved to Florida mid-year, you may owe part-year state tax to your previous state

Florida Crypto Tax: Key Statistics

  • Florida state income tax rate: 0%
  • Florida state capital gains tax: 0%

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Florida tax cryptocurrency?

Florida has no state income tax and no state capital gains tax, making it one of the most tax-friendly states for crypto investors. Only federal tax applies.

Does Florida tax crypto mining or staking?

No state tax applies. Federal ordinary income tax and self-employment tax still apply to mining and staking rewards.

Does Florida have any crypto-specific tax rules?

Florida has been notably crypto-friendly. The state doesn't impose additional crypto taxes, and it has actively courted crypto businesses to relocate there. However, crypto businesses may owe Florida corporate income tax or sales tax depending on their activities.

Do I need a Florida crypto CPA if there's no state tax?

Federal crypto tax is complex enough on its own. A Florida-based crypto CPA can also help with business formation, multi-state filings, and IRS representation.
