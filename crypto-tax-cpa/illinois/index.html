Frequently Asked Questions

Does Illinois tax cryptocurrency differently than the federal government?

No. Illinois conforms to federal treatment of crypto as property. Every trade, swap, or purchase with crypto is a taxable event. The state applies a 4.95% flat income tax to crypto capital gains.

Does Illinois tax staking and mining rewards?

Yes. Staking and mining rewards are treated as ordinary income at fair market value when received, taxed at Illinois' 4.95% flat rate. They then establish a new cost basis for future capital gains calculations.

Can I use crypto losses to offset Illinois income?

Yes, up to $3,000 per year in net capital losses can offset ordinary income, with the remainder carried forward. Illinois conforms to federal capital loss rules.

Do I need to report crypto on my Illinois state return?

Yes. The Illinois Department of Revenue requires you to report all capital gains and losses, including crypto. Illinois has been increasingly cross-referencing IRS data to catch unreported crypto gains.
