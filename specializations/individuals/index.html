You may not need a CPA

If you only bought and held crypto on a single exchange, your situation is probably simple enough for crypto tax software. Koinly, CoinTracker, and similar tools generate IRS-ready reports for $49–$199.

Consider a CPA if any of these apply:

  • You used more than 2 exchanges or wallets
  • You have DeFi, NFT, or staking activity
  • You received airdrops or mined crypto
  • You never filed and need to catch up
  • You received an IRS notice
  • Your gains exceed $50,000
