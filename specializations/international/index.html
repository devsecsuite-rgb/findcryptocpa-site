International crypto tax is its own specialty

If you're a US citizen or green card holder living abroad, or a non-US resident with US crypto activity, you face reporting requirements that most CPAs never touch. FBAR and FATCA alone carry penalties starting at $10,000 per violation — per account, per year.

What you're required to report

  • FBAR: Foreign exchange accounts holding crypto may require FinCEN Form 114 if aggregate value exceeds $10,000
  • FATCA: Form 8938 reporting for foreign financial assets over threshold amounts
  • Expatriation: Exit tax applies if you renounce citizenship with over $2M in net assets

What can help

  • Treaty benefits: US tax treaties with 65+ countries may reduce double taxation on crypto gains
  • Streamlined Filing: If you missed prior years, the Streamlined Domestic or Foreign Offshore Procedures may allow catch-up with reduced penalties
