If you received an IRS notice about crypto

The IRS has dramatically increased crypto-related enforcement. Letters like CP2000, CP2501, and the newer 6174 and 6174-A notices are automated, but ignoring them creates escalating penalties and interest. A CPA experienced in crypto audit defense can respond on your behalf.

What to do first

  • Do not respond directly: Anything you say can be used against you — let a CPA handle the correspondence
  • Gather your history: All exchange exports, wallet addresses, and prior year returns
  • Understand the notice type: CP2000 is a proposed adjustment; 6174 is an educational letter; 6174-A is a soft warning

What a CPA can do

  • Penalty abatement: First-time penalty abatement may eliminate penalties if you have a clean 3-year history
  • Amended returns: Many cases are resolved by filing corrected returns rather than going through audit

Crypto Audit Defense: Key Statistics

  • IRS crypto audits increased 52% in 2025 vs 2024
  • IRS sent 758% more crypto warning letters in a 60-day period in 2025
  • Average cost of IRS crypto audit representation: $5,000–$25,000
  • Percentage of crypto audits that result in additional tax owed: 71%
